Ethereum’s Pectra upgrade reshaped how validators manage balances. The next upgrade, Glamsterdam, will materially improve exit liquidity. The proposals included in Glamsterdam address two areas of particular interest to institutional stakers: the architecture of block building, and the speed at which validators can exit large staked ETH positions.
